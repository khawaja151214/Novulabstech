FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What industries does NovuLabs specialize in?
Banking & Fintech, Healthcare, Government, Manufacturing, and E-Commerce. Our deepest expertise is in AML, CFT, and FMU Pakistan compliance for regulated financial institutions.
How do I start a project with NovuLabs?
Book a free consultation — a 45-minute discovery call with a senior architect, zero commitment. We listen first, then advise on the right approach for your specific needs.
Do you offer post-launch support and maintenance?
Yes. Tiered SLA packages from standard business-hours support to 24/7 platinum tiers with 4-hour guaranteed response. All enterprise clients get a dedicated account manager.
Are your solutions compliant with FMU Pakistan regulations?
Absolutely. We have live deployments at multiple SBP-regulated institutions — GOAML integration, STR/CTR reporting, AML/CFT screening, all under FMU and FATF frameworks.
Can you work alongside our existing team?
Yes. Staff augmentation, co-development, and full outsourcing — we adapt to your stack, tools, and methodologies. Many clients embed our engineers alongside their in-house teams.
