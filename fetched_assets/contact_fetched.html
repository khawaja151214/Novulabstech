FAQ

Common Questions

What is the minimum project size NovuLabs works with?
Typically $15,000 USD for a standalone module or MVP. Full enterprise platforms start from $50,000. We focus on end-to-end delivery — not hourly freelance work.
Do you sign NDAs before discussions?
Absolutely. We sign mutual NDAs before any substantive technical discussion. Your IP and competitive information are fully protected from the start.
Can you work alongside our existing in-house team?
Yes. We offer staff augmentation, co-development, and full outsourcing. Our engineers adapt to your existing stack, tools, sprint ceremonies, and communication preferences.
What compliance frameworks do you support?
FATF, FMU Pakistan, SBP, FCA, HIPAA, HL7 FHIR, PCI-DSS, ISO 27001, GDPR, and Mastercard/Visa network compliance. Deep in-house expertise in Pakistani, UAE, and UK/EU regulatory requirements.
Do you offer fixed-price or time-and-material contracts?
Both. Fixed-price for well-defined, stable requirements. Time-and-material for evolving enterprise platforms. We recommend the best model after the discovery call based on your specific situation.
